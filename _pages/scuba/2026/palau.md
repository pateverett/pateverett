I dove Palau with [Palau Dive Adventures](https://www.palaudiveadventures.com), a top-tier operation with exceptional guides and deep knowledge of the islands and dive sites. Palau is world-famous for its shark aggregations, dramatic wall dives, and historic WWII wrecks, and the itinerary covered some of its most iconic sites — including Blue Corner, Blue Holes, German Channel, Chandelier Cave, and the Ulong Channel.
